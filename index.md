# Privacy Policy

**Pavlov Quit Smoke**

Last updated: 13 February 2026

Pavlov Quit Smoke (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Overview

Pavlov Quit Smoke is a behavior-based smoking reduction and cessation app.

The app operates without traditional account registration (no email/password login).

Each user is identified by a randomly generated device-based identifier.

We do not sell personal data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Device & Technical Information

When you first open the app, we generate a unique anonymous user ID linked to your device.

We may collect:

- Anonymous User ID (UUID)
- Device token
- Platform (iOS / Android)
- Device model & OS version
- App version & build number
- Timezone
- Push notification subscription ID (if notifications enabled)

This data is used strictly for app functionality, diagnostics, and notification delivery.

---

### 2.2 App Usage Data

We collect behavior-based app data such as:

- Smoking reduction plan settings (wake time, sleep time, target date)
- Daily schedule status (smoked, skipped, auto-logged)
- Progress statistics (cigarettes avoided, time saved, money saved)
- Badge/achievement status
- App screen interactions (analytics)

This data is used to:

- Generate your personalized reduction schedule
- Track progress
- Improve product experience
- Measure feature effectiveness

---

### 2.3 Push Notification Data

If you enable notifications:

- We store your push subscription ID via OneSignal
- We track notification delivery status (sent, failed, dropped)

We do not access your contacts, SMS, or call history.

---

### 2.4 Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized usage events such as:

- Screen views
- Onboarding step completion
- Feature usage
- Conversion metrics

We do not collect:

- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Exact location
- Contacts
- Photos (unless explicitly uploaded by user for optional features)

---

### 2.5 Photos (Optional Feature)

If you upload a “last cigarette photo”:

- The image is stored securely
- It is not shared publicly
- It is used only inside your personal account experience

---

## 3. Legal Basis (GDPR / KVKK)
